About Logi Play

Logi Play is your lightweight, browser-first hub for retro gaming and secure, local emulation. Load legally-owned ROMs directly in your browser — no uploads, no external storage — just local playback powered by EmulatorJS and Play!.js.

Emulator (Logi-EMU)

Quickly load and play ROMs in the browser. Supports GBA, SNES, NES, N64, and more.

Games Directory

Future curated directory for browser-friendly homebrew/free games.

Privacy & Security

ROMs remain local — nothing is uploaded. Only use ROMs you legally own.
